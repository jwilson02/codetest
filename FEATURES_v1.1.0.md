## Keepers of the Flame Features

### 1. 💜 Breach Helper (`Ctrl+Shift+U`)

**Purpose:** Track all Breach mechanic progress in the Keepers of the Flame league.

**Features:**
- **5 Breach Types Tracked:**
  - Physical (Tul) - Cold breach
  - Fire (Xoph) - Fire breach
  - Lightning (Esh) - Lightning breach
  - Chaos (Chayula) - Chaos breach
  - Uul-Netol - Physical breach

- **Breach Hives:** Track completion of all 5 Breach Hive locations
  - Tul's Hive - The Frozen Bastion
  - Xoph's Hive - The Molten Depths
  - Esh's Hive - The Storm Nexus
  - Chayula's Hive - The Void Sanctum
  - Uul-Netol's Hive - The Earthen Citadel

- **Ailith Protection:** Toggle and track Ailith's protective status
- **Unstable Breaches:** 45-second timer with wave tracking
- **Splinter Counter:** Track all splinter types, shows breachstone readiness (100 splinters)
- **Graftblood Tracking:** Monitor league currency for Genesis Tree
- **Session Stats:** Breaches completed, rewards earned, time played

**How to Use:**
1. Press `Ctrl+Shift+U` to open
2. Select current breach type from dropdown
3. Toggle Ailith protection if active
4. Track unstable breach timer
5. Mark hives as completed when cleared
6. View splinter progress and breachstone readiness

**Popular Combinations:**

## Trading & Economy Features

### 5. 💬 Trade Whisper Manager (`Ctrl+Shift+W`)

**Purpose:** Manage trade conversations and respond quickly to buyers.

**Features:**
- **Auto-Parse Whispers:** Copy trade whisper from POE, auto-extracts:
  - Player name
  - Item name
  - Price
  - League
  - Stash location (tab, left, top)
  - Asynchronous trade indicator (3.27)

- **10 Quick Responses:**
  - "1 sec" / "One moment"
  - "inv" / "Invite sent"
  - "ty" / "Thanks!"
  - "sold" / "Already sold"
  - Custom templates

- **Trade History:** Last 50 trades with search
- **Session Stats:** Trades/hour, total trades, completed count
- **Status Tracking:** Pending, Completed, Cancelled

**How to Use:**
1. Copy trade whisper from POE (`Ctrl+C` on message)
2. Press `Ctrl+Shift+W` to open manager
3. Paste whisper in input box
4. Click quick response button
5. Message automatically copied to clipboard
6. Paste in POE (`Ctrl+V`) to send
7. Mark trade as Complete/Cancel when done

**Whisper Formats Supported:**
- Standard: `@From PlayerName: Hi, I would like to buy your [Item] listed for [Price] in [League]`
- Async Trade: `@From PlayerName: (Asynchronous trade) Hi, I would like to buy...`
- Bulk: `@From PlayerName: Hi, I'd like to buy your 100 Chaos Orb for my 20 Divine Orb in League`

  - 💰💰💰 Very High (Divine Orb, etc.)
  - 💰💰 High (Chaos, Chromatic)
  - 💰 Medium (Jeweller's, etc.)

- **Difficulty Ratings:** ● Easy, ●● Medium, ●●● Hard
- **Favorites:** Star recipes for quick access

**How to Use:**

3. View recipe requirements

## Progression Tracking Features

### 7. 🃏 Divination Card Tracker (`Ctrl+Shift+D`)

**Purpose:** Track divination card collection and farming.

**Features:**
- **22+ Cards:** Complete database including:
  - **Ultra-Rare:** The Doctor, Apothecary, House of Mirrors
  - **Popular:** Humility, The Nurse, Abandoned Wealth
  - **3.27 New:** A Dab of Ink (Voidborn boss reward)

- **For Each Card:**
  - Stack size and current progress
  - Reward (what it gives)
  - Drop locations (maps/acts)
  - Farming strategies
  - Market value (chaos equivalent)
  - Rarity tier

- **Collection Stats:** Total cards, completion %, total value
- **Filters:** By rarity (mirror-tier, ultra-rare, rare, uncommon, common)
- **Sort:** By name, value, completion, rarity

**How to Use:**
1. Press `Ctrl+Shift+D` to open
2. Find your card in the list
3. Click +/- to adjust stack count
4. Expand card for drop locations and farming guide
5. Track progress bars to full stack
6. See total collection value in chaos

**Best Farming Locations:**
- **The Doctor** - Burial Chambers, Spider Forest
- **Humility** - Blood Aqueduct (Act 9)
- **The Nurse** - Tower Map
- **Apothecary** - Crimson Township

- Value ratings and usage strategies

- Ember/Ichor tiers (Lesser → Greater → Grand → Exceptional)
- Eldritch Chaos/Exalt/Annul

#### **Crafting Strategies:**

- Tailwind Boots (Est: 100-500c)

# World Lore - Chronicles of the Amnesiac Hero

## The Realm of Aethermoor

Welcome to Aethermoor, a fantasy realm where magic is real, dragons have anxiety, and apparently no one knows how to name things without being either overly dramatic or disappointingly literal.

---

## The Age System

Aethermoor's history is divided into Ages, each marked by catastrophic events, heroic deeds, or really bad administrative decisions.

---

## The First Age - The Dawn of Inconvenience (Year 0-500)

### The Creation Myth (Probably Inaccurate)

According to legend, the world was created when the Primordial Being sneezed while crafting reality. This explains several geographical anomalies, including:
- The Inexplicably Floating Islands
- The Mountain Range That Looks Like a Face
- The Ocean That's Somehow Square

Most scholars agree this myth is absurd. The prevailing theory is that reality was formed through complex magical resonance patterns. This is somehow considered less ridiculous.

### The First Peoples

The earliest inhabitants of Aethermoor were:

**The Elder Races:**
- **Firstborn Elves** - Immortal, wise, insufferably smug about both facts
- **Primal Dwarves** - Masters of stonework and holding grudges (both with equal skill)
- **Ancient Dragons** - Intelligent, powerful, and surprisingly into poetry
- **The Fae Folk** - Tricksters who thought linear time was "boring"

### The Magic Discovery

Magic was discovered accidentally when an elf named Thalindor tried to light a cooking fire and instead created a small sun. This incident:
- Gave everyone third-degree burns
- Revolutionized energy production
- Made Thalindor famous (he hated the attention)
- Established the first rule of magic: "Read the spell BEFORE casting"

### The First Cities

The Elder Races built magnificent cities:

**Silvermoon Spire** (Elven)
- Towers that touched the clouds
- Architecture described as "aggressively aesthetic"
- No right angles anywhere (elves found them "vulgar")
- Still standing today, somehow

**Irondeep Hold** (Dwarven)
- Carved into mountain heart
- 47 levels of tunnels
- Population: Unknown (dwarves bad at census)
- Motto: "We Dug Too Deep But We're Not Admitting It"

**Wyrmrest Peaks** (Dragon)
- Mountain range claimed as "nesting grounds"
- Hoarding culture began here
- Weekly poetry slams every Wednesday
- Surprisingly progressive dragon rights legislation

**The Wandering Grove** (Fae)
- Forests that moved when bored
- Geography extremely frustrating for cartographers
- Time moved differently inside (hours could be days, or backwards)
- Now mostly sealed away (for everyone's sanity)

## The Second Age - The Era of Heroes (Year 500-1200)

### The Age of Adventuring

Humans arrived! No one knows from where. They showed up one day with ambitious attitudes and disappointingly short lifespans. The Elder Races were unimpressed.

**Humanity's Contribution:**
- Invented the concept of "rush hour"
- Shortened spell incantations (lazy but effective)
- Popularized the pub
- Created the adventuring economy

### The First Heroes

The Second Age saw the rise of legendary heroes:

**The Platinum Company** (Year 645)
- Five heroes who saved the world from the Demon King
- Became so famous they couldn't go anywhere without autograph requests
- Retired to open a chain of restaurants ("Platinum Platters")
- Restaurants failed spectacularly

**Saint Meridia the Slightly Judgmental** (Year 712)
- Founder of the Holy Order
- Defeated the Undead Lord Varesh
- Wrote extensive religious texts (very long, very dry)
- Canonized against her explicit dying wishes

**Grimjaw the Surprisingly Gentle** (Year 788)
- Orc warrior who united the tribes
- Wrote bestselling cookbook
- Introduced concept of "aggressive hugging"
- Statue depicts him frowning (he was actually smiling)

### The Founding of Kingdoms

Human kingdoms emerged:

**Kingdom of Aetheron** - The "Good" Kingdom
- Founded by King Aetheron the First (creative naming was not his strength)
- Capital: Shining City (literally shines, it's very annoying at night)
- Motto: "Justice, Honor, and Reasonable Tax Rates"
- Still exists, currently ruled by King Alderon the Reasonable

**Shadowmere Dominion** - The "Evil" Kingdom
- Founded by Lord Malvern the Definitely Evil
- Capital: Blackspire (they committed to the aesthetic)
- Motto: "Villainy With Vision"
- Surprisingly well-run bureaucracy

**Republic of Merchant States** - The "Neutral" Kingdom
- Founded by merchants tired of good vs. evil drama
- Capital: Coinworth (subtle)
- Motto: "Our Prices Can't Be Beat (Probably)"
- Controls 90% of trade routes

### The Great Magical Academies

Magic became institutionalized:

**Celestial College** (Founded Year 823)
- Premier magical institution
- Acceptance rate: 2% (very exclusive)
- Notable alumni: 12 archmages, 3 world-savers, 1 supervillain (we don't talk about Marcus)
- Tuition: Ruinous

**School of Practical Sorcery** (Founded Year 856)
- Affordable alternative to Celestial College
- Motto: "Magic for the Working Mage"
- Teaches useful spells like "Summon Coffee" and "Minor Cleaning"
- Graduates get real jobs

## The Third Age - The Shadow Wars (Year 1200-1500)

### The Demon Incursions

Demons broke through from the Nether Realm:

**First Incursion** (Year 1205)
- Demons invaded
- Were immediately confused by paperwork requirements
- Spent 3 months navigating customs
- Defeated through bureaucratic attrition

**Second Incursion** (Year 1247)
- Demons learned from mistakes
- Brought lawyers
- Actually got pretty far
- Defeated by coalition of kingdoms

**Third Incursion** (Year 1290)
- Final demon invasion
- Massive war, countless casualties
- Heroes emerged
- Demon portal permanently sealed
- Both sides agreed to yearly non-invasion treaty (mostly honored)

### The Age of Dark Lords

Somehow, humans started producing Dark Lords at alarming rate:

**Lord Malachai the Grim** (Year 1301-1305)
- Wanted to block out sun (vitamin D deficiency suspected)
- Defeated by crop of new heroes
- Remembered as "pretty standard" Dark Lord

**The Lich Queen Morwenna** (Year 1340-1347)
- Undead sorceress
- Excellent administrative skills
- Her evil empire had great healthcare
- Heroes felt bad defeating her (citizens rioted when benefits ended)

**Dreadlord Xarathis** (Year 1389-1394)
- Had a dragon army
- Dragons unionized mid-campaign
- Defeated by his own workforce demanding better conditions
- Currently in prison writing memoir

### The Council of Races

After centuries of conflict, all races formed the Council:

**Purpose:**
- Prevent world-ending catastrophes
- Mediate disputes
- Regulate dangerous magic
- Organize annual potluck

**Members:**
- Elven Representative (always punctual, annoyingly so)
- Dwarven Representative (always late, unapologetic)
- Human Representative (always different, short lifespans)
- Dragon Representative (takes up three chairs)
- Observer from Other Realms (confused by everything)

**Achievements:**
- Prevented 47 wars
- Established magical regulations
- Created International Trade Standards
- Failed to agree on lunch location (ongoing 200-year debate)

## The Fourth Age - The Age of Prophecy (Year 1500-1700)

### The Prophet Zarathos

A human prophet named Zarathos began having visions:

**His Prophecies Included:**
- "The One Who Cannot Remember Shall Make Everyone Else Wish They Could Forget"
- "When Seven Moons Align, Order Pizza In Advance"
- "The Darkness Beyond Light Seeks the Light Beyond Darkness" (this one's actually important)
- "Invest in Copper Futures" (people who listened got rich)

### The Creation of Power Crystals

Archmage Cornelius (yes, THAT Cornelius) created three power crystals:

**Red Crystal of Righteous Fury**
- Imbued with heroic essence
- Side effect: Compulsive noble speeches
- Hidden in Caverns of Moral Ambiguity

**Blue Crystal of Cunning Shadows**
- Imbued with stealth and guile
- Side effect: Dramatic rooftop brooding
- Hidden in Caverns of Moral Ambiguity

**Purple Crystal of Chaotic Mischief**
- Imbued with reality-bending chaos
- Side effect: Life becomes sitcom
- Hidden in Caverns of Moral Ambiguity

**What Happened to Cornelius:**
- Attempted to create fourth crystal
- Experiment went wrong
- Died, became ghost
- Haunted those caverns for 200 years
- Eventually relocated to Dungeon of Perpetual Inconvenience

### The First Cycle

The prophecy began:

**Year 1645 - First Hero**
- Adventurer awakened with no memory
- Found crystals
- Fought Dark Lord Malevolus (yes, he's that old)
- Failed to stop The Void Between
- Reality partially unraveled, then reset
- No one remembers this except Cornelius (ghost memories persist)

### Subsequent Cycles

The cycle repeated multiple times:
- Different heroes, same amnesia
- Always fought Malevolus
- Never quite succeeded
- Reality kept resetting
- Each cycle left faint echoes
- This explains déjà vu

## The Fifth Age - Modern Times (Year 1700-Present Day)

### The Current Era

**Year 1702 - Now**
Present day of the game. This cycle's hero (the player) awakens.

### The State of the World

**Political Situation:**
- Kingdoms maintain uneasy peace
- Malevolus consolidating power (again)
- Trade routes flourish
- Adventuring is booming industry

**Social Issues:**
- Growing wealth inequality
- Magical education access
- Monster rights movement
- Dungeon worker unionization

**Technological Advances:**
- Magitech communication crystals
- Enchanted transportation
- Magical refrigeration (revolutionary for food storage)
- Printing press (spreading dangerous ideas like "literacy")

### Current Threats

**Lord Malevolus "The Dreadfully Terrible"**
- Has been Dark Lord for 300+ years
- Actually trying to prevent greater threat
- Uses villainy as cover for preparations
- Really committed to the aesthetic

**The Void Between**
- Eldritch entity beyond reality
- Seeks to unmake existence
- Grows stronger each cycle
- Can only be sealed, not destroyed
- Main antagonist (Malevolus is red herring)

**The Watchers**
- Secret organization
- Monitors prophecy cycles
- Manipulates events from shadows
- Morally ambiguous
- May be helpful or hindering (player determines)

## Geography of Aethermoor

**The Central Kingdoms**
- Temperate climate
- Majority human population
- Where most story takes place
- Excellent roads (dwarven engineering)

**The Elderwood**
- Ancient forest
- Elven territory
- Trees are semi-sentient
- Time moves strangely
- GPS doesn't work

**The Iron Mountains**
- Dwarven holds
- Rich in minerals
- Excellent beer production

- Volcanic region
- Dragon territory
- Actually beautiful if you're fireproof
- Hot springs popular tourist destination

**The Merchant Seas**
- International waters
- Controlled by no kingdom
- Pirates vs. Merchants eternal conflict
- Some islands are actually giant turtles

**The Forbidden Lands**
- Where Fae used to rule
- Reality unstable
- Time doesn't work right
- Enter at own risk
- Makes for great vacation photos (if you return)

## Magic System

### Types of Magic

**Elemental Magic**
- Fire, Water, Earth, Air
- Most common type
- Taught in schools
- Can blow up spectacularly if done wrong

**Divine Magic**
- Granted by gods/faith
- Healing and protection focus
- Requires genuine belief
- Gods are suspiciously quiet about theology debates

**Arcane Magic**
- Pure magical energy
- Most versatile
- Requires study and intelligence
- Makes you sound pretentious

**Shadow Magic**
- Stealth and illusion
- Not inherently evil (despite reputation)
- Very useful for parties
- Makes birthday parties AMAZING

**Chaos Magic**
- Unpredictable and wild
- High risk, high reward
- Not taught in schools (for good reason)
- Results may vary

**Blood Magic**
- Forbidden in most kingdoms
- Uses life force
- Powerful but dangerous
- Definitely not worth it (usually)

### Magical Creatures

**Dragons**
- Intelligent, powerful
- Hoard treasure (psychological need)
- Some have anxiety
- Protected species

**Unicorns**
- Rare, magical
- Surprisingly aggressive
- Will headbutt trespassers
- Not as gentle as stories suggest

**Phoenix**
- Rebirth cycle
- Very warm to hug (literally)
- Terrible at keeping appointments
- "I'll be there unless I die" (unhelpful)

**Griffins**
- Half eagle, half lion
- Proud and noble
- Also very cat-like (naps frequently)
- Guard important locations

**Demons**
- From Nether Realm
- Not all evil (most are just doing jobs)
- Complex bureaucracy in home realm
- Annual non-invasion treaty mostly holds

## Religion and Beliefs

### The Pantheon

**Solara, Goddess of Light**
- Represents hope and justice
- Worshipped by paladins
- Very judgy
- Excellent dental coverage for followers

**Luneth, God of Night**
- Represents mystery and dreams
- Worshipped by rogues and artists
- Chill about most things
- Never answers prayers during business hours

**Terramor, The Earth Mother**
- Represents nature and growth
- Worshipped by druids and farmers
- Environmentally conscious
- Very disappointed in everyone

**Pyros, God of Forge**
- Represents creation and destruction
- Worshipped by blacksmiths
- Has anger management issues
- Makes great weapons though

**The Forgotten God**
- Name lost to time
- Represents memory and loss
- Connected to prophecy
- Possibly responsible for amnesia curse
- Needs better PR

### Philosophical Schools

**The Determinists**
- Believe fate is fixed
- Prophecy must be fulfilled
- Very depressing at parties
- "Why bother" is their motto

**The Free Willers**
- Believe choice is everything
- Reject prophecy
- Optimistic but naive
- "We forge our destiny" is their motto

**The Pragmatists**
- Believe in whatever works
- Don't care about theology
- Popular with merchants
- "Can I profit from this?" is their motto

## Economy and Trade

### Currency

**Gold Dragons** (Gold pieces)
- Standard currency
- 100 copper = 10 silver = 1 gold
- Dragon emblem (dragons get royalty)

**Adventuring**
- Huge economic driver

- Enchanted goods
- High demand

## The Void Between - The Real Threat

### Origins (Unknown)

The Void exists outside reality. It is:
- Not evil (evil requires intention)
- Not alive (life requires existence)
- Simply hungry for reality
- Growing stronger each cycle
- The true final boss

### How It Works

- Feeds on existence itself
- Erases things from having ever been
- Creates time loops to feed repeatedly
- Responsible for the cycles
- Can only be sealed, never destroyed
- Requires sacrifice to seal

### Warning Signs

- Reality feeling "thin"
- Déjà vu increasing
- Memories feeling uncertain
- Time anomalies
- Things disappearing retroactively
- Existential dread
